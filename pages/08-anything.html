Your birthday wish

If I could give you one thing for your birthday…

I would give you the ability to see yourself through my eyes.

The person who makes me laugh.

The person I look at and think,"I hope he knows how much he is loved."

The person whose presence can make my mind feel a little quieter.

The person I keep choosing, every day.

Because if you could see yourself through my eyes,
you would understand why you mean so much to me.
