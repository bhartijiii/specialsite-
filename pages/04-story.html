Not a timeline. Just the story.

Our little story

Before 2024

If I'm being honest, I don't really count all those years as our togetherness.There was a time when we were both lost, not even knowing what we were doing or where we were going. We had our reasons to leave,we tried to let go, and sometimes we actually did.But somehow, we never completely lost each other.Maybe we needed all those confusing years to finally understand what we mean to each other. And maybe that's what makes our story ours- it was never perfect but somehow, we always found our way back.

Then in 2024

Not dramatically. Just quietly, the way the best things tend to arrive — and then suddenly I was paying attention.We had our little struggles in between, we fought, we misunderstood each other, but none of it was big enough to make us give up. We talked, we understood and somehow kept coming back to each other. Slowly, things started feeling more real, more settled, and more ours. Now, when I look at us, I just smile and think- How did we come this far?

The little things

The silly talks. The random questions. Our video calls, the little updates about our day.The comfort of talking to you like it was never new to begin with.

The memories

Not many yet. But every one of them is one I've kept, carefully, without meaning to.

And here we are

Still early. Still learning each other. And somehow, already, one of my favourite parts of life.
